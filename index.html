The Thesis

1850s to 1950s

Public Health

Population-scale intervention through civic infrastructure: sanitation, vaccines, maternity care. A new layer of health value, built from scratch.

1950s to 2020s

Hospital Health

Clinician-led systems focused on specialisation and treatment. Hospitals, pharmaceuticals, modern medicine. Enormous capital deployed. Enormous returns generated.

2020s onwards

Personalised Health

Distributed, data-driven, patient-enabled care. Prevention, prediction, personalised treatment pathways. The transition is underway. The infrastructure companies are being built now.
