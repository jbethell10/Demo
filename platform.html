Havilland Growth — The Execution Platform

Havilland Growth is an independent business. It has its own team, its own clients, and commercial model. Healthcare scaling expertise is available to portfolio companies and to the market.

Havilland Growth works with healthcare companies that need more than capital to scale. It provides commercial scaling, regulatory navigation and healthcare system expertise to portfolio companies and other healthcare businesses operating in complex markets.

We have particular depth across the UK, Europe, Israel and the Gulf, supporting companies entering and scaling across complex healthcare markets.

Capital and Execution. Working as One.

When Havilland Active Ventures invests, Havilland Growth becomes available as an execution partner. Portfolio companies receive capital, board-level support and expertise in commercial scaling, regulatory navigation and healthcare systems.

Havilland Growth also generates independent deal flow. Its work across healthcare systems and markets surfaces companies that become investment candidates for the fund.

The two businesses reinforce each other while operating independently.

Better outcomes for portfolio companies. Stronger deal flow for the fund. Advantages a capital-only model cannot replicate.
