Our Thesis

Healthcare is entering its third structural transition. The companies building the infrastructure for that transition are the investment opportunity.

Healthcare Has Never Been Static

Healthcare has reinvented itself three times in recorded history. Each time, a convergence of social pressure, scientific advance, and political will created a new infrastructure layer. Each time, the companies that built and scaled that infrastructure captured disproportionate returns.

1850s – 1950s

Public Health

Population-scale intervention through civic infrastructure: sanitation, vaccines, maternity care. A new layer of health value, built from scratch.

1950s – 2020s

Hospital Health

Clinician-led systems focused on specialisation and treatment. Hospitals, pharmaceuticals, modern medicine. Enormous capital deployed. Enormous returns generated.

2020s →

Personalised Health

Distributed, data-driven, patient-enabled care. Prevention, prediction, personalised treatment pathways. The transition is underway. The infrastructure companies are being built now.

Each era created new winners. Each era rewarded those who identified the infrastructure need early and backed the companies capable of building it at scale. We believe the third era will be no different.

The Clinician-Centric Model Cannot Scale

Modern health systems face pressures they were never designed to absorb. Rising chronic disease, ageing populations, clinician shortages and constrained budgets are not temporary challenges.

The argument is not that existing systems failed. The context around them has changed faster than their design. The institutions are not broken. They are simply insufficient.

This creates a structural premium for scalable clinical value.

Demand

More chronic conditions and longer lifespans are increasing complexity per patient.

Capacity

Clinician time and institutional throughput remain constrained in many major systems.

Expectation

Patients increasingly expect personalization, speed and measurable outcomes.

Each era rewards those who identify the infrastructure need early and back companies capable of building it at scale.

The Investment Opportunity

A new generation of healthcare companies is building the systems that allow clinical value to be delivered without proportionate increases in clinician time. AI diagnostics, health data platforms, genomics and remote monitoring are extending the reach of healthcare systems and changing how care is delivered.

These are not incremental improvements. They are the infrastructure of the next era of healthcare delivery. The companies that build and scale them will capture the same structural returns that hospital builders and pharmaceutical manufacturers captured in the previous era.
